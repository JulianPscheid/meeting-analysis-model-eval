Extract all strategic, architectural, or significant business decisions made during the meeting from the supplied transcript.

Return one JSON array of objects, where each object represents a single decision and has exactly these keys:
- "decision": A clear statement of what was decided (string).
- "owner": The person who made the decision or owns its execution (string). If unclear, use null.
- "context": A brief explanation of why the decision was made (string).
- "alternatives_considered": An array of strings representing other options discussed but ultimately rejected. If none were discussed, return an empty array.

Do not include trivial decisions (like scheduling another meeting). Do not invent decisions.
Emit no characters before or after the JSON array.
